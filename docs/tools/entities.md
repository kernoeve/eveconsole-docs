# Players & NPCs

A searchable browser for EVE entities — the players who inhabit New Eden and the NPCs defined in the game's static data — that lets you look any of them up by name.

Open it from the left sidebar under **Corp / Interactions**.

## What it shows

The browser has two sides.

**Players** — every pilot, corporation, and alliance, shown with their kills, losses, and member counts.

**NPCs** — agents, NPC corporations, and factions drawn from the SDE (the game's static data).

## Using it

- **Search by name** to look up any entity, on either side.
- **Read a player's record** — kills, losses, and member counts for a pilot, corporation, or alliance.
- **Look up NPC data** — agents, NPC corporations, and factions from the SDE.

## Notes

- The kills and losses shown here are the same activity recorded in [Killmails](killmails.md); open that tool for the individual killmail detail.
- Entity names are clickable across the app's tools, so you can jump to an entity's record from wherever a name appears.
- The NPC side comes from the bundled SDE and needs no ESI authorization.
