# Structure Browser

A list of player-owned structures across New Eden, built from ESI's public structure list rather than only the ones you have personally visited or docked at.

Open it from the left sidebar under **Assets**.

## What it shows

Every player structure that ESI publishes in its public list, which is a much larger set than the structures you would otherwise know about — hundreds of facilities rather than the roughly 140 you would accumulate just from docking and visiting.

For each structure ESI describes, the app records the details it returns (such as the structure's name, location, and services). Some structures have no public details, and for those ESI will not describe what they are or what they provide.

## Using it

- **Browse the wider structure set** to find a facility that would not appear from your own travel history alone.
- **Link a structure to an industry park** so a park entry can point at a real facility (see [Indy Parks](../industry-parks.md)).
- **Record a fitting by hand** for a structure ESI will not describe. Entering its fit lets the app still know the services and rigs that structure provides, even though no public details are available.
- **Record which way a structure faces.** ESI doesn't say where a structure's undock points, so pick it under **Faces toward** in the structure's editor: another structure in the same system, or one of its stargates, listed nearest first with the distance. With nothing picked, the closest stargate is taken as the default. The [Worklist](worklist.md)'s in-system hauling uses it to order its stops, so a freighter undocks toward its next stop instead of turning round. It's saved as soon as you pick.

## Notes

- The public structure list comes from ESI and is only as current as your last sync; it reflects what ESI chooses to publish, not the complete set of every structure in the game.
- Structures with no public details will not describe themselves — recording a fitting by hand is how you supply that information.
- Use with [Indy Parks](../industry-parks.md), where a park entry can be pointed at a structure from this browser.
