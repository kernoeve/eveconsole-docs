# Game & Chat Logs

Viewers for the EVE client logs your PC writes locally — the **Game Log** and the **Chat Log** — that let you search your history and import past logs in bulk.

Open it from the left sidebar under **Tools**.

## What it shows

Two viewers over the log files EVE saves on your computer:

- **Game Log** — your searchable game-log history.
- **Chat Log** — your searchable chat-log history.

Chat logs from intel channels are additionally parsed into *sightings*: who was seen, where, and in what hull. Those sightings surface on the [Universe Map](universe-map.md) overlays and on each system's Intel tab.

## Using it

- **Search your history** in either the Game Log or the Chat Log.
- **Import past logs in bulk** to bring older history into the viewers.
- **Read intel sightings** on the [Universe Map](universe-map.md) overlays and the per-system Intel tab, derived from your chat logs.

## Notes

!!! note
    Duplicate messages are recognised across characters and machines, so importing a second computer's logs does not double-count the same sighting.

- This tool reads the log files EVE writes locally on your PC (the Gamelogs and Chatlogs folders); it does not fetch anything from ESI.
- Parsed sightings feed the intel overlays described under [Universe Map](universe-map.md).
